            The Eisenhower Matrix

            Named for Dwight D. Eisenhower, who said: "I have two kinds of problems, the urgent and the important. The urgent are not important, and the important are never urgent." Stephen Covey popularized the method in The 7 Habits of Highly Effective People.

            Every task is rated on two axes and falls into one of four quadrants:

            • Do: urgent and important. Do it yourself, soon.
            • Schedule: important, not urgent. Plan time for it. This is where meaningful work lives.
            • Delegate: urgent, not important. Hand it off if you can.
            • Eliminate: neither. Drop it, or park it.

            This app plots each task as a dot so you can see at a glance where your attention is going. There are two separate matrices, Work and Home, so different parts of life stay apart; click the active tab to rename it. Everything is stored in this browser, and you can also keep a save file on disk from the menu.
